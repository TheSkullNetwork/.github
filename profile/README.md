# Welcome to The Skull Network 💀

The Skull is a community for developers, security researchers, and tech enthusiasts who live at the intersection of coding and cyber security. Whether you are building full-stack apps, digging into malware, or running OSINT investigations, this is a space to share tools, collaborate on open-source projects, and level up together.

---

### 🌐 Connect With Us
The core of our network lives on Discord. Join our community to collaborate on projects, discuss offensive/defensive operations, share scripts, and connect with like-minded minds.

### 🛠️ Our Focus Areas
* **Full-Stack Development:** Building secure, scalable applications and automated workflows.
* **Cyber Security:** Exploring offensive operations, defensive engineering, and malware analysis.
* **OSINT & Recon:** Open-source intelligence methodologies and asset discovery.
* **Open Source Tools:** Developing public resources, scripts, and frameworks for the community.
